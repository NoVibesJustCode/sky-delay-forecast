Delay Index (1 − OTP)

The Delay Index shown on each airport pin is derived from the Operational Time Performance (OTP) metric, a standard in the aviation industry.

Delay Index = 1 − On-time flightsTotal operated flights

A flight is considered on time if its departure delay is 15 minutes or less (the IATA standard threshold).

  • 0.0 — All recent flights departed on time
  • 0.5 — Half of recent flights were delayed > 15 min
  • 1.0 — Every recent flight was significantly delayed

Computed from the most recent historical flight data per airport.
